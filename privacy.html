Velora privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Velora is a free browser game made by Elango. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Playing as a guest

Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you play on. It is never uploaded. Other players can't see it.

Signing in with Google (optional)

  • If you choose "Continue with Google", sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The game receives your name, profile picture and an account ID. It does not use your email address, and it never sees your password.
  • Your game save (cash, businesses, purchases and other game data) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account, so you can continue on any device where you sign in.
  • Database rules allow only you to read or change your save. Other players can't see it.
  • A random device ID is stored with your save so the game knows which device saved last.
  • Google processes this data under Google's privacy policy and the Firebase privacy terms.

What the game loads

The game, its images and its fonts are served from this site. The Firebase sign-in and database code is loaded from Google (www.gstatic.com) when you play.

Deleting your data

  • In the game, open Settings and choose "Delete cloud save" to remove your save from Google Cloud.
  • Choose "Start over" to reset your game, and clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything on this device.
  • To disconnect Google, visit your Google account connections and remove Velora.
