Velora privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Velora is a free browser game made by Elango. It has no server and no database. Everything below happens inside your own browser.

Your game progress

Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you play on. It is never uploaded anywhere. Other players can't see it, and nor can we.

Signing in with Google (optional)

  • You can play as a guest without signing in.
  • If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, profile picture and an account ID with the game, in your browser only.
  • The game uses them to label your save and show your picture. It keeps a separate save for each Google account on your device.
  • Your email address and password are never stored, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The sign-in itself is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

What the game loads

The game, its images and its fonts are served from this site. The Google sign-in button is loaded from accounts.google.com when sign-in is available. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

  • In the game, open Settings and choose "Start over" to reset your save.
  • To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To disconnect Google, visit your Google account connections and remove Velora.

Questions

Open an issue on the game's page at github.com/Elxngo/velora.
